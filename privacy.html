Privacy Policy

Odoo apps by ABBAS · last updated 17 October 2026

This policy covers the Odoo apps published by ABBAS (S M Abbas S, Karachi, Pakistan) on the Odoo Apps Store, including Omnichannel Inbox, which connects an Odoo database to the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API), Facebook Messenger, Instagram messaging and the Telegram Bot API.

Who controls the data

The apps run inside the Odoo database of the business that installs them. That business decides which channels to connect and is responsible for the messages and contacts stored in its own Odoo. ABBAS does not operate a server for the apps, does not receive copies of messages and has no access to a customer's Odoo database.

What the apps process

  • Messages, photos, files and delivery or read statuses sent to the business through the connected channels.
  • The sender's name, phone number or platform ID, needed to show the conversation and link it to a contact.
  • Pricing information that Meta includes in status updates (whether a message was charged).

This data is sent by Meta or Telegram directly to the business's own Odoo and stored there. It is used only to let the business answer its customers, assign conversations to its staff and report on response times.

Sharing

Replies written in Odoo are sent to the customer through the official API of the channel (Meta or Telegram). The apps do not sell data, do not use it for advertising and do not send it to any other party.

Data deletion

Messages and contacts are deleted by the business in its own Odoo (conversations, messages and contacts can be deleted there at any time). If you wrote to a business that uses these apps and want your data removed, contact that business directly. For any question about this policy or a deletion request you cannot resolve with the business, email abbasodolution@gmail.com and we will help within 30 days.

Security

Incoming webhooks are checked with the platform's signature before they are accepted. Access tokens are stored in the business's Odoo and are visible only to inbox managers.
