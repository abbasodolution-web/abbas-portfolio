Process

From idea to go-live in five steps

  1. 1

    Discover

    We talk through the process and the pain. I ask the questions that save you rework later.

  2. 2

    Design

    A short written plan: screens, data, rules, price and dates. You approve before I code.

  3. 3

    Build

    Small, reviewable steps on a staging copy, so you can see progress every few days.

  4. 4

    Test

    Automated tests plus a walk-through with your users. Edge cases are checked, not hoped for.

  5. 5

    Support

    Go-live help, a handover note and a fix window for anything we missed.
